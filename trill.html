Campaign Primer ▾

Key Reference

Setting: Asterra, a land of kingdoms, hardy folk, and forgotten histories. The Kingdom of Valedorn is the central power, ruled from Kingsfall.

The First Sundering: Over 1,000 years ago, tears opened across the world. Through them came the Shreeden, fleeing horrors that erased people from existence. The people of the kingdom fought alongside the Shreeden and sealed the tears. Then the Shreeden disappeared. Nobody knows if the Sundering really happened or is myth.

The Faith of the Veil: Oldest religious tradition. Preserves ancient songs, ceremonies, and relics from the Sundering. Respected but quietly doubted.

The Northern Border: Fighting along the Ashen Frontier. Soldiers disappear. Villages are abandoned. Strange reports from survivors. The Crown says it's hostile forces. The truth is worse.

Key Locations: Kingsfall (capital), The Ashen Frontier (northern border), The Shattered Woods and Ragged Crossing (where things vanish), Silvermere Basin (farmland), Grayhaven (Trill's tavern territory), Sableport (where Trill's family sailed from), The Silent Bastions (ancient ruins, scattered), The Hollow Steps (Ironspine Mountains).

Open Mysteries: Are the tears reopening? What happened to the Shreeden? Why are people being erased again? Does the Crown know? Are the old songs the key to sealing the tears?
The Tale of the First Sundering A story every child in the kingdom grows up hearing. No one agrees on exactly how it happened. Some say the stars vanished. Some say the moon turned crimson. Others claim the earth itself cried out. What every version agrees upon is this: Over a thousand years ago, the world nearly ended. In those ancient days, strange tears opened across the land. The sky split apart as though made of glass, revealing another world beyond. Through those impossible openings came creatures unlike anything the people of the kingdom had ever seen. They were called the Shreeden. Every painting and carving depicts them differently. Some show them as tall, glowing figures with enormous eyes. Others portray them as winged spirits or ghostly beings wrapped in blue light. No one knows which, if any, are accurate. The old stories insist they were not invaders. They were fleeing. Behind them came horrors from beyond the tears, monsters so terrible that many legends refuse to describe them directly. Some tales say they devoured cities. Others claim they erased people so completely that even their loved ones forgot they had ever existed. Whatever the truth, the people of the kingdom stood beside the Shreeden. Together they fought a war unlike any before or since. In the end, the enemy was driven back. The tears were sealed. The kingdom was saved. Then the Shreeden disappeared. From those events grew the Faith of the Veil, one of the oldest religious traditions in the kingdom. Its priests teach that the First Sundering truly happened and that the sacrifice of the Shreeden should never be forgotten. Today, life goes on. For years, fighting has continued along the northern border. Officially, the crown says hostile forces threaten the frontier, and every season more soldiers are sent to defend the realm. News from the border is often confusing, villages abandoned, patrols disappearing, strange accounts from frightened survivors, but such tales are easily dismissed as exaggerations born from the hardships of war. If there is any truth to those legends, it has long since been lost to time. Or so everyone believes.
